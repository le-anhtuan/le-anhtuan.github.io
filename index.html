Mechanics and Materials Science

Anh Tuan LE

I am seeking a postdoctoral opportunity in an international, interdisciplinary research environment. At École Polytechnique in France, I developed experimental expertise in electro-chemo-mechanical coupling by cycling lithium-ion half-cells while applying mechanical loads to their silicon electrodes.

My doctoral research examined how processing and microstructure influence the mechanical properties of nanostructured composites. I aim to build on this experience through further postdoctoral research and pursue a long-term academic career.

Research

Electro-chemo-mechanics

Silicon electrodes

At the Laboratoire de Mécanique des Solides, I applied mechanical loads during electrochemical cycling and used operando measurements to investigate how stress affects the electrochemical response of amorphous silicon electrodes in lithium-ion half-cells.

Read the journal article

Processing and fracture

Fibre-reinforced composites

During my doctorate, I studied how to integrate vertically aligned carbon nanotubes into continuous-fibre laminates and how that manufacturing process affects interlaminar fracture behaviour.
