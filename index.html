Mechanics & materials science

Anh Tuấn Lê

I study how materials respond to mechanical loading, from silicon electrodes during battery cycling to fibre-reinforced composites. My work combines materials processing, mechanical characterisation and operando experiments.

01

Research

Electro-chemo-mechanics

Silicon electrodes

At the Laboratoire de Mécanique des Solides, I combined electrochemical cycling with operando mechanical loading to investigate how stress affects the electrochemical response of amorphous silicon electrodes in lithium-ion half-cells.

Read the journal article

Processing & fracture

Fibre-reinforced composites

During my doctorate, I studied how to integrate vertically aligned carbon nanotubes into continuous-fibre laminates and how that manufacturing process affects interlaminar fracture behaviour.
